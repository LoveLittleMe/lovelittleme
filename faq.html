About the product

Love Little Me is an AI-powered emotional healing platform that creates personalized animated videos for adults processing childhood wounds. You select the wound that resonates most with your experience, upload a childhood photo, and answer 3 guided questions. We use that information to generate a healing script written specifically for you, animate your younger self in a warm Ghibli-inspired scene, and deliver a video of a loving parent figure speaking your healing words directly to you.

No. Love Little Me is not therapy and is not a substitute for professional mental health care. We are not licensed clinical doctors, therapists, or medical professionals, and we do not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment of any kind.

Love Little Me is a creative and emotional wellness experience — a personalized video designed to help you connect with and offer compassion to your younger self. Many people find it a meaningful complement to their existing healing work, but it is not clinical care.

If you are experiencing significant mental health challenges, we encourage you to seek support from a licensed mental health professional.

Videos are typically delivered within 24–48 hours of completing your order and answering your guided questions. You will receive an email notification the moment your video is ready, and it will also be available to download from your confirmation page.

There is no subscription and no recurring charges.

Your photo

The best photos are clear, well-lit images of your face as a child — ideally between ages 3 and 10. Your face should be clearly visible and facing forward. Solo photos tend to produce better results than group photos.

Old printed photos scanned with your phone work perfectly. Any photo from your phone or computer is accepted — JPG, PNG, or HEIC format, up to 10MB.

If we cannot process your photo — because the image is too blurry, too dark, the face is not clearly visible, or the file doesn't meet our requirements — we will email you with a clear explanation and simple instructions for resubmitting a different photo. Your order will not be cancelled and you will not be charged again.

We accept JPG, PNG, and HEIC files up to 10MB. This covers virtually every photo taken on an iPhone, Android, or digital camera. If you're scanning an old printed photo, save it as a JPG at normal quality — that will work perfectly.
