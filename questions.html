Your words to little you

Before you choose your child, take a moment with these three questions. They are shaped around your wound, and your answers help us build your video around your story. There are no right answers, and you can write as little or as much as you like. Whatever you share stays private and is used only to create your video.
Your reflection is held safely. Your answers save automatically as you write.
How to answer these
There are no right answers, and you don't need perfect words. Write a full paragraph, a few phrases, or a single line — whatever comes honestly. If a question feels too hard, or you're not sure what to say, it's completely okay to skip it and keep going. Nothing here is graded, and nothing is ever shared.
Take your time. If a question feels like too much right now, you can leave it and keep going — even a single sentence helps us hold your story with care.
